play.

Games, puzzles & playful interaction

Games you play with your hands, your feet and the world around you: ring puzzles you turn on paper, a phone or a watch, floor tiles that light up underfoot, darts in augmented reality, and art hidden at real places in a city.

DartsAR, Geodex and Stakco are mine. The iTiles games and Kiwrious Play are contributions to the Augmented Human Lab.
